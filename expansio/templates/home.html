What I focused on building

Atomic registration flow

OTP verification over Gmail SMTP, wrapped in transaction.atomic() so a failed step never leaves orphaned data in the database.

Decimal currency arithmetic

All money values use Python's Decimal type instead of float. No IEEE 754 rounding surprises in balances or reports.

EMI burden engine

Monthly and weekly recurring loan deductions computed dynamically by date range. The dashboard auto-deducts active EMIs from your net balance.

Per-user data isolation

Database-level UniqueConstraint on (user, name, type) tuples. Every query filters by the logged-in user.
