Key Engineering Highlights

verified_user Atomic registration flow

OTP verification over Gmail SMTP, wrapped in transaction.atomic() so failed steps never leave orphaned records.

calculate Decimal currency arithmetic

All financial figures use Python's Decimal type instead of float, eliminating IEEE 754 precision issues.

account_balance EMI burden engine

Monthly and weekly recurring loan deductions computed dynamically by active date ranges, adjusting disposable balance automatically.

lock Per-user data isolation

Database-level UniqueConstraint on (user, name, type) ensures isolated records across accounts.
